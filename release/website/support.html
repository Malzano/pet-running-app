PAWPACE

A little help.

How does my egg hatch?

Finish and save workouts. Your egg hatches after 30 active minutes, then grows from baby to adult after 180 more. Up to 60 minutes a day count toward growth and genes. Rest days preserve progress.

How do genes work?

Your mix of activities shapes ordinary coats: Endurance gives mint, Power peach, Calm lavender, and Explorer sky. Each egg also has a saved 5% Moonlight and 1% Aurora inheritance chance, revealed at hatching. Reopening the app and exercising harder do not reroll it.

How do I explore the garden?

Drag to rotate, pinch to zoom, or use the zoom buttons. Reset returns to the starting view. After hatching, tap your pet or use Snack and Play. Rest pauses roaming.

Can I use PawPace without an Apple Watch?

Yes. Use the iPhone workout timer. A paired Apple Watch can add live workout readings when its sensors and permissions support them. Missing readings appear as a dash.

What if the app closes during a workout?

A saved phone workout checkpoint can be reviewed when you return. It opens paused, and time while the app was closed does not count. Review or discard it before starting a different phone workout.

Where is my workout history?

Open Home → Settings → Workout journal. The journal includes workouts saved with this version onward; it does not import older Apple Health workouts. Removing the journal leaves your pet’s growth and Apple Health records in place.

How do I change permissions?

Open Home → Settings for Health access information or to open iPhone Settings. Manage individual Health categories in the Health app. The workout timer works without Health and location access.
