PAWPACE

Your movement.
Your little world.

PawPace is a workout companion that helps a virtual pet grow through movement. Buddy, daily quests and Planner work without an account. Optional friends and clubs use Sign in with Apple and a separate PawPace service. There are no advertising or analytics services.

Information on your devices

Your pet’s name, appearance, growth, genes, rewards, daily movement totals, check-ins, streak evidence and claimed mystery rewards are saved on your device. Your paired Apple Watch receives companion and workout updates. Home Screen widgets use the shared companion snapshot.

Workout summaries may include activity, dates, active time, distance, available heart rate and energy measurements, and earned rewards. Protected local files store these summaries, interrupted-workout checkpoints, pending completion records, and companion progress. These files are excluded from system backups. Earlier backups made with older versions may still contain older saved data.

Apple Health and permissions

You choose which Health categories PawPace may use. With permission, the app reads relevant workout, distance, active energy, and heart rate data and can save workouts, distance, and energy to Apple Health. PawPace does not sell Health data or use it for advertising. Apple Health maintains its own copies under your Health and iCloud settings.

You can use the workout timer without granting Health or location access. Missing sensor readings remain empty. You can manage Health access in the Health app and location access in iPhone Settings.

Location

During suitable outdoor workouts, location estimates distance and pace. The route appears in the finish recap with start and finish marked. Tracking stops when you pause or finish. Raw routes are not included in the saved workout journal or uploaded to a PawPace server. Apple’s system services, including maps, operate under Apple’s own privacy terms.

Optional friends and clubs

The service stores your Apple account identifier, a generated alias, encrypted Apple refresh token, private friendships, club memberships and plans. It does not request your Apple name or email. Club activity-day sharing begins off. When enabled, five credited movement minutes may contribute a dated achievement to that club, without the underlying workout or step totals.

Friend requests require a private code and acceptance. You may explicitly post a workout's activity, completion date, active duration and optional distance. Only friends connected before the post was shared can view it. Your route, heart rate, calories, photos, pet name and species are not posted. The feed displays up to 100 recent posts from the last 30 days. Posts remain stored until you remove them or delete your account. Preset cheers contain no free-form messages.

Delete a post from its menu or remove/block a friend from Club → Friends. Removing a friend ends access to each other's posts after the device syncs. Deleting a post removes its result fields; a minimal identity tombstone prevents delayed duplicate requests from restoring it. Deleting your Club account removes its service data and closes hosted clubs. Signing out removes the local account cache and unsent actions, while the remote account remains. Local account caches and queues use protected storage excluded from backups, and the session credential uses device-only Keychain storage.

The service is not configured in the current development build. Before publication, the publisher must supply hosting details, logging practices, backup expiration and deletion procedures. These controls describe the implemented live database; no unconfigured backup-erasure guarantee is made.

Retention and deletion

Your local companion and workout journal stay on the device until removed. In Settings, Delete workout journal removes the journal and cancels pending journal writes. It does not remove your pet, undo earned progress, or delete Apple Health records. Workouts in Apple Health can be managed separately in the Health app. Temporary completion records are removed when their saves finish; interrupted workout records are removed when completed or explicitly discarded.

Removing the app removes its private container. Shared containers and paired-device copies may remain while related components are installed. Other non-fitness app preferences can be included in your device’s system backup.
