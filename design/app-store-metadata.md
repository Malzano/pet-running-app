## Description

Turn everyday movement into a little friendship.

Your PawPace journey begins with a mystery egg. Walk, run, stretch, lift or enjoy another workout to discover the little friend waiting inside. Keep moving together as your new friend grows from baby to grown-up.

**Grow at your own pace**

Thirty progress minutes hatch your egg. Another 180 minutes help your baby grow up. Up to 60 minutes a day count toward growth and adventures, and rest days keep every bit of progress.

**A companion shaped by you**

Your mix of endurance, strength, calming and exploratory activities shapes your pet's traits and coat. Some eggs hold a rare coat surprise, revealed at hatching. Faster pace and higher heart rate do not improve those odds.

**A cozy place to return to**

Spend time in your pet's garden, explore its decorations, and celebrate the movement you have done together.

**A world to discover**

Grown companions help you find more mystery eggs, with every friend keeping a place at home. Choose gentle expeditions, find decorations and memories, and build a weekly rhythm with room for rest. Share your discoveries with cards that let you keep or reveal the surprise.

**Everyday activity, if you choose**

Enable shared Apple Health steps and new workouts to count movement recorded outside PawPace. One hundred steps earn one game progress minute. We count the larger daily total of workout minutes or step progress, up to 60 minutes, so the same movement is not added twice.

**Work out with iPhone and Apple Watch**

Choose an activity, record your session and follow the available measurements. With permission, PawPace reads relevant Apple Health data and saves completed workouts. Apple Watch provides live workout measurements; the phone also supports timed workouts. Widgets and Live Activities keep your companion and session close by.

Buddy and Planner work without an account. Daily adventures add gentle streaks and earned mystery gifts. Optional friends and clubs use Sign in with Apple and only the activity days or workout results you choose to share. Pet progress stays on your devices, with a companion experience on your paired Apple Watch. Measurement availability depends on your device, activity and permissions. PawPace is a fitness companion, not a medical service.

**Plan a gentle week**

Schedule movement and rest in Planner, set optional reminders, and let matching completed workouts check off your plans. Play three little games, choose expedition paths, arrange earned keepsakes and save memories with your companion.

## Review notes draft

Buddy, Planner and the workout timer require no login, paid unlock or external backend. Club requires Sign in with Apple and the separately configured service. The current local build has no Club service URL; replace this note with verified reviewer instructions after provisioning, and do not submit a build advertising unavailable shared features. The first launch creates one persistent mystery egg. Start a workout from Home or the Workout tab; choose Yoga for a simple phone timer flow. Finish and save a short session to see progress, XP and a journal entry. Health access is optional for this timer flow. Outdoor location and Watch Health permissions are requested where needed.

The pet hatches after 30 active minutes and reaches adulthood after 210 total credited minutes, with a 60-minute cap per finish day. This is ordinary progression. Species and rare inheritance are saved at egg creation; rarity is revealed at hatching. The app uses workout duration and type for cosmetic growth and traits. Heart rate, pace, energy and distance do not determine rarity or speed growth.

The Apple Watch companion records workouts and synchronizes state with the paired iPhone. Testing sensor measurements, Health saves and Watch session recovery needs a paired physical Watch and iPhone. Multisport transitions are manual. The app does not claim Apple's automatic multisport recognition or sport-specific coaching features.

Open Settings for the guide, privacy explanation, pet naming and local journal controls. Deleting journal history does not undo pet rewards or remove copies saved in Apple Health. Health permissions and Health records can be managed in Apple's Health app.
